# Minor damage shouldn't cost the whole deposit

## Background

Right now a return is either "fine" (full deposit back) or "damaged" (we keep the whole
deposit and the item goes to maintenance). Last week a customer lost their entire 400.00
deposit on the Mirrorless Camera because of a scratch on the lens cap, and left a one-star
review. The front desk has been asking for something in between for a while.

## What we want

Support **partial deposit refunds** when an item comes back with minor damage, so the
customer only pays for what was actually damaged and gets the rest of the deposit back.

- Minor damage should not cost the whole deposit.
- Serious damage still keeps the full deposit, as today.
- The return receipt should show how much of the deposit was refunded and how much was
  withheld (it already has fields for that).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Returns support partial deposit refunds for minor damage.
- [ ] The receipt reflects the refunded and withheld amounts.
- [ ] Current behaviour for undamaged and seriously damaged returns stays the same.
